## 1. Research background

Metal halide perovskite solar cells have reached a certified power conversion efficiency of 25% [1], yet their operational stability remains the main obstacle to commercialization [2]. Moisture ingress is a widely reported trigger of irreversible degradation of the perovskite lattice [3]. Epoxy-based encapsulation extends the T80 lifetime from 300 h to 1000 h at 45% relative humidity [4].

## 2. Related work

Film-growth studies compare spin coating, vapor-assisted growth and blade coating on the same footing: spin-coated films show larger grain size but poorer uniformity [5], vapor-assisted growth reaches micrometer-scale grains with a surface roughness of 18 nm [6], and blade coating at a coating speed of 5 mm s-1 yields an efficiency of 19.8% [8]. These routes trade film uniformity against process throughput, and none of them resolves the moisture sensitivity of the resulting lattice [5][6][8].

Device-level engineering follows a second line: solution-processed devices with suppressed hysteresis reached an efficiency of 21.4% [7], while phenethylammonium iodide passivation raised the open-circuit voltage to 1.12 V [9]. Both studies report single-device gains and do not separate the passivation effect from the growth route [7][9].

Degradation and characterization work provides the third line of evidence: operational stability metrics are defined and compared across device stacks [2], moisture-driven degradation pathways are mapped for methylammonium lead iodide [3], and epoxy encapsulation is shown to extend the T80 lifetime of modules [4]. Lattice strain in perovskite films is quantified by X-ray diffraction [10], and the broader space of characterization techniques for hybrid perovskites is reviewed [12]. Certified efficiency progress is tracked at the community level [1], while low-light operation for indoor applications has been assessed separately [11].

## 3. Research gap

The combination of vapor-assisted growth and phenethylammonium iodide passivation has not been reported [5][6][9]. Few studies quantify moisture-driven lattice strain at the grain boundary [10][12]. Whether the improved T80 lifetime measured at 45% relative humidity is retained at 85% relative humidity remains unknown [4][11].

## 4. Contributions

We propose a two-step route that combines vapor-assisted growth with phenethylammonium iodide passivation. The optimized device reaches 23.5% power conversion efficiency, an open-circuit voltage of 1.12 V, and a short-circuit current density of 23.5 mA cm-2. The T80 lifetime improves to 1000 h at 45% relative humidity and to 420 h at 85% relative humidity.
